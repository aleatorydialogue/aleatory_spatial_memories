# Aleatory Spatial Memories — Project Brief

## Purpose

Aleatory Spatial Memories is a consumer-facing reality capture business focused on preserving meaningful moments, people, objects, and places as high-fidelity spatial memories.

The business should not primarily market Gaussian splats, photogrammetry, neural rendering, or other reconstruction technologies.

Those technologies are the backend.

The product is the ability to preserve a meaningful moment in richer dimensional form than ordinary photography or video.

A useful framing is:

> Photography captures a view.
> Video captures a sequence.
> Aleatory Spatial Memories captures the space itself.

The goal is to make advanced 3D and 4D capture understandable and desirable to ordinary people who may have no technical interest in how the reconstruction is produced.

---

## Core Customer Promise

We help people preserve important moments in a form they can revisit, explore, display, archive, and eventually experience through future media.

Possible use cases include:

* families and children at specific stages of life
* weddings and anniversaries
* grandparents and multi-generational family gatherings
* pets
* meaningful homes or rooms before a move
* performances and celebrations
* personal objects with emotional significance
* unusual life events
* memorial and legacy preservation
* artistic or cultural moments

The customer is not buying a file format.

They are buying a richer record of something they care about.

---

## Long-Term Vision

Aleatory Spatial Memories may eventually operate through two primary capture systems.

### Mobile Capture

A portable capture service that brings an appropriate camera system to the customer.

Depending on the subject and scale, this could involve:

* synchronized camera arrays
* distributed camera fleets
* static multi-view capture
* dynamic or volumetric capture
* spatial audio
* larger crowd or event capture systems

The mobile system should make high-end spatial capture accessible outside of a fixed studio.

### Memory Dome

A future dedicated studio containing a fixed 360-degree camera array for high-quality capture of people, objects, groups, and performances.

The Memory Dome should function both as a technical capture system and as a distinctive physical experience associated with the brand.

It should feel more like visiting a futuristic portrait studio than visiting a technical scanning facility.

---

## Deliverables

Customers may receive both digital and physical forms of their Spatial Memory.

Potential digital deliverables include:

* interactive 3D or volumetric reconstruction
* web-viewable version
* high-quality archival reconstruction
* rendered video versions
* source imagery where appropriate
* standardized export formats
* future AR/VR-compatible versions
* spatial audio where applicable

Potential physical deliverables include:

* a distinctive branded archival USB or digital keepsake
* NFC-enabled or otherwise interactive physical artifacts
* 3D-printed representations of the captured scene
* stylized sculptures derived from the capture
* objects with varying levels of abstraction, detail, color, or resolution
* future display technologies such as light-field or spatial displays

A major product principle is that a digital memory should ideally have a meaningful physical anchor rather than existing only as a download link.

## Initial Product

The first version of the business should be much narrower than the long-term vision.

An appropriate initial offer is a limited early-capture program.

For example:

### Founding Captures

A small number of selected customers receive an experimental spatial capture using the current mobile reconstruction pipeline.

The goals are to:

* test the real capture workflow
* learn what customers value
* identify practical limitations
* create strong example reconstructions
* generate testimonials and case studies
* produce marketing footage
* improve the reconstruction pipeline
* determine viable pricing and service packages

The initial site should therefore prioritize lead generation and applications rather than pretending that every long-term capability already exists.

Do not make unsupported claims.

## Positioning Principles

### Sell the memory, not the technology

Technical details should be available for curious users, but they should not dominate the primary customer experience.

Avoid leading with terms such as:

* Gaussian splatting
* NeRF
* photogrammetry
* neural rendering
* point clouds
* radiance fields

Those terms may appear in technical or educational sections when useful.

### Emotional value comes first

The website should communicate why preserving a moment spatially matters.

The core emotional idea is that current photographs and videos capture only limited projections of an event.

Spatial capture creates a richer record that may become increasingly valuable as AR, VR, spatial displays, and future reconstruction technologies improve.

### Preserve future optionality

The reconstruction technology will change.

The company should not define itself around any single current technique.

Today's backend may use Gaussian splats or related reconstruction methods.

Future systems may be substantially different.

The durable product is preserved reality.

## Brand Direction

Working name:

**Aleatory Spatial Memories**

Other naming may be explored, but agents should not rename the project casually.

Any naming proposal should preserve the ideas of:

* memory
* space
* preservation
* future access
* emotional significance
* unusual or experimental technology

The tone should feel:

* human
* curious
* slightly futuristic
* artistic
* premium without being sterile
* technically sophisticated without sounding technical
* emotionally meaningful without becoming sentimental or clichéd

Avoid generic startup language.

Avoid excessive futurist jargon.

Avoid making the customer feel as if they need technical knowledge to understand the product.

## Relationship to aleatory.ai

Aleatory Spatial Memories is an operating project under aleatory.ai.

aleatory.ai is the broader experimental company focused on using advanced AI systems to build unusual products, services, and creative projects.

Spatial Memories is intended to become one of the first serious market-facing applications built from Aleatory's technical experimentation.

---

## AI-First Operating Model

Aleatory Spatial Memories should itself be built as an AI-first company.

The project is also an experiment in maximizing the leverage of one human founder through AI agents, automation, and agent swarms.

AI systems may assist with:

* research
* product development
* software development
* design
* marketing
* customer intake
* content production
* documentation
* operations
* analytics
* internal decision support

The founder remains responsible for judgment, taste, priorities, customer relationships, and real-world execution.

The goal is not merely to use AI tools.

The goal is to explore what a company designed around AI-native operations looks like from the beginning.

---

## Build-in-Public Strategy

The process of building this company should itself produce content.

Development may be streamed and documented through the existing **Holding the String** channel.

Holding the String focuses on the broader process of a human directing increasingly autonomous AI systems.

The Spatial Memories business should remain customer-facing and emotionally oriented.

This creates two related narratives:

1. **Aleatory Spatial Memories:** the product and the memories being created.
2. **Holding the String:** how one person is using AI agents to build the company behind it.

Raw development streams, swarm activity, technical failures, decisions, and experiments belong primarily under Holding the String.

Finished customer-facing capture content belongs primarily under the Spatial Memories brand.

## Initial Website Goal

The first major deliverable is a functional consumer-facing website.

The website should explain the concept clearly enough that a nontechnical visitor can understand why they might want a Spatial Memory.

The initial website should likely include:

### Home

A strong emotional introduction and visual explanation of spatial memory capture.

### What Is a Spatial Memory?

Explain the difference between photography, video, and spatial capture in simple terms.

### Capture a Memory

Explain the early mobile capture service and provide a way to apply or express interest.

### What You Receive

Explain digital reconstruction, archival files, keepsakes, and possible physical artifacts.

### The Memory Dome

Present the long-term studio vision without implying that it currently exists if it does not.

### How It Works

An optional deeper technical explanation for interested users.

### Building Aleatory

A lightweight connection to the AI-first build process and Holding the String project.

## Current Phase

This is an experimental v0.

Priorities are:

1. Clarify the consumer proposition.
2. Build a compelling website.
3. Create real capture examples.
4. Find early users.
5. Learn what customers actually value.
6. Improve the capture and delivery pipeline.
7. Develop a repeatable mobile service.
8. Document the entire process.
9. Use AI agents wherever they meaningfully increase founder leverage.

Do not overengineer the first version.

The purpose of v0 is to move from technical experimentation into real market contact.

---

## Key Strategic Principle

The company should be resilient to changes in underlying reconstruction technology.

Crowd capture systems, Gaussian splats, dynamic splats, neural reconstruction, future camera systems, generative reconstruction, spatial audio, physical fabrication, and technologies that do not yet exist may all become capabilities of this business.

They do not each need to become separate businesses.

They can all serve one durable mission:

**preserve meaningful pieces of reality so people can experience them again in forms that were previously impossible.**
