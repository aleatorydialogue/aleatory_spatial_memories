# Brand context

## Brand role

Aleatory Spatial Memories is the customer-facing service. It should make advanced capture feel emotionally legible and desirable without requiring technical literacy.

It operates under aleatory.ai, the broader experimental company. Holding the String tells the behind-the-scenes story of a human building with increasingly autonomous AI systems. Customer-facing capture stories belong primarily to Spatial Memories; raw development process, swarm activity, and technical failures belong primarily to Holding the String.

Cross-links should be lightweight and intentional. The build-in-public narrative must not make customers or intimate memories feel like experimental content.

## Core idea

Photography captures a view. Video captures a sequence. A Spatial Memory captures enough of the space to let a person return to it from more than one fixed viewpoint.

This comparison is a conceptual aid, not a claim that every aspect of a moment or space can be preserved.

## Launch product line

**Remembering Presence** describes the central promise: access to the felt spatial presence of one's own past events. Use it as a product-line name, not as a guarantee of literal presence, perfect reconstruction, or universal volumetric-video delivery. Aleatory Spatial Memories remains the company and service brand.

## Personality

- Human and attentive
- Curious and quietly surprising
- Artistic, with technical confidence beneath the surface
- Slightly futuristic but grounded in present human meaning
- Premium through care, restraint, and craft—not sterility or exclusivity

## Experience principles

1. Lead with the memory and why it matters.
2. Show spatiality whenever possible instead of explaining it abstractly.
3. Make experimental status and limitations feel honest, not apologetic.
4. Create a sense of care before, during, and after capture.
5. Treat future possibility as invitation, never certainty.
6. Give every demanding visual or interactive experience a graceful accessible fallback.

## Visual working direction

The visual system should pair intimate, authentic subject matter with a restrained sense of dimensionality. Consider depth, parallax, orbit, changing viewpoint, negative space, subtle materiality, and careful motion. Avoid sci-fi interface clichés, glowing grids, generic AI imagery, sterile scanner aesthetics, and effects that compete with the captured subject.

Authentic captures should ultimately anchor the identity. Concept imagery must be labeled or used in a way that cannot be mistaken for a delivered customer result.

## Naming

“Aleatory Spatial Memories” is the working name. Naming alternatives require an explicit founder-led decision and should preserve memory, space, preservation, future access, emotional significance, and experimental character. Domain, trademark, pronunciation, and consumer comprehension remain open research questions.
