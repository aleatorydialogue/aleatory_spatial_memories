# Claims and terminology

This document guards public copy against confusing customers or promising capabilities that have not been demonstrated. It is not legal advice; customer-facing terms and material claims require appropriate review.

## Lead with

- Spatial Memory
- Remembering Presence, as the product line
- Preserve a meaningful person, place, object, or moment
- Revisit or explore from more than one viewpoint
- Experimental or limited Founding Captures program
- Mobile capture, when it accurately describes the present workflow
- A richer record than a single fixed view

Prefer concrete, calm language. Explain unfamiliar ideas with examples or visuals.

## Use only with evidence and qualification

- **High fidelity:** specify what was measured and under which conditions.
- **Archival:** state the delivered formats, retention arrangement, and limits; do not imply permanent preservation.
- **Future-compatible / future-proof:** name current exports and avoid guarantees about unknown platforms.
- **Volumetric video, 4D, or dynamic capture:** use only when the demonstrated service preserves meaningful change over time, not merely a moving view of a static scene. Do not describe it as the result of every Founding Capture.
- **360-degree:** use only for experiences or coverage that actually meet that description.
- **Private or secure:** tie the statement to specific implemented controls.
- **Physical keepsake:** distinguish available products from concepts under exploration.
- **AR/VR compatible:** name tested devices, formats, and limitations.

## Keep out of primary marketing

Gaussian splats, NeRFs, neural rendering, point clouds, radiance fields, photogrammetry, camera-array specifications, and other backend terms. They may appear in an optional technical explanation when accurate and useful.

For the current technical explanation, it is accurate to say that the project currently uses Gaussian splatting as a backend reconstruction technique. Do not turn that implementation detail into the product identity.

## Do not claim without proof

- Permanent, lossless, exact, complete, or perfect preservation
- Guaranteed compatibility with future devices or media
- Uniform results across subjects, motion, lighting, or environments
- A Memory Dome that currently exists or accepts bookings
- Deliverables, turnaround, pricing, service areas, or retention periods not operationally established
- Ownership, confidentiality, deletion, or privacy protections not reflected in contracts and systems

## Status labels

Use consistent labels where ambiguity could mislead:

- **Available now** — operational and repeatedly deliverable
- **Founding Captures experiment** — available selectively with disclosed uncertainty
- **In development** — active work, not purchasable
- **Future vision** — directional aspiration with no delivery promise

Every published case study should identify what was actually captured and delivered. Separate service consent from permission to publish images, names, testimonials, or behind-the-scenes material.
