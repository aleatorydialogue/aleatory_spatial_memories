# Product context

## Mission

Preserve meaningful pieces of reality so people can revisit them in richer forms than conventional photography or video allows.

Customers are not buying a reconstruction method or file format. They are buying a richer record of someone or something they care about, with the possibility of exploring, displaying, archiving, and experiencing it through future media.

## Current product stage

Aleatory Spatial Memories is an experimental v0. The key product line is **Remembering Presence**: meaningful events and scenes captured from multiple viewpoints so customers can return to a spatial representation of their own past. Depending on what the current workflow can faithfully support, an experience may be delivered as a static spatial scene or volumetric video.

The immediate offer is a limited **Founding Captures** program in Connecticut using the founder's current mobile reconstruction workflow. It will include only a small number of custom scenes and may be free or offered at a low introductory price in exchange for candid feedback. Exact eligibility, price, deliverables, and publication rights must be agreed per capture. It is not yet a broadly available, standardized service.

The program should help the project:

- Test real capture and delivery workflows
- Learn which memories and outcomes customers value
- Establish practical subject, setting, and quality limits
- Create consented examples, testimonials, and case studies
- Discover viable service packages and pricing

Until field evidence exists, the website should invite interest or applications and describe the experimental nature of the offer.

## Customer outcome

A successful engagement should make a customer feel that a meaningful subject or moment has been preserved with a sense of presence unavailable in a single photograph or fixed video. The experience around capture and delivery matters alongside technical fidelity.

Potential deliverables from the brief are a menu of future possibilities, not a v0 commitment. The exact initial package remains to be validated.

## Candidate audiences

The brief identifies families, couples, grandparents, pets, meaningful homes and rooms, performances, celebrations, personal objects, memorials, and cultural moments. These audiences should not all be treated as equal launch segments.

Before final homepage messaging, choose one primary and at most one secondary early audience based on:

- Access to suitable participants
- Reliability of the current pipeline for that subject
- Strength and safety of demonstrable examples
- Emotional clarity of the outcome
- Operational feasibility and willingness to pay

Memorial, minor, and private-home use cases require especially careful consent and tone.

## Product horizons

### Current

- Experimental multi-camera mobile capture in Connecticut
- Static spatial reconstruction using Gaussian splatting as the current backend technique
- Volumetric video only where the specific capture workflow supports it
- Lead/application intake
- Learning-oriented delivery defined case by case

### Near-term, subject to validation

- A repeatable mobile capture service
- Clearly packaged digital outputs
- A meaningful physical anchor or keepsake
- Private, accessible ways to revisit delivered memories

### Long-term vision

- Larger mobile camera systems and dynamic or event capture
- Spatial audio and broader physical artifacts
- The fixed-array Memory Dome studio
- Experiences on emerging AR, VR, and spatial displays

Long-term items must be labeled as vision, exploration, or future possibility.

## Required product discovery

Before public launch, establish a capability matrix for supported subjects, motion, group size, setting, lighting, capture duration, geographic range, processing time, output formats, viewing requirements, quality variability, failure handling, retention, and recapture policy.

Also define the Founding Captures program's capacity, selection process, price or compensation, customer deliverables, turnaround expectation, publication permission, and exit criteria.
