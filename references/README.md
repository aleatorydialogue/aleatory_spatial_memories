# Reference material

This area stores curated external evidence and inspiration used to inform product decisions. Reference material is not automatically approved project truth.

## Categories

- `brand/` — relevant visual, verbal, and experiential precedents
- `competitors/` — comparable offerings and positioning
- `customer-research/` — consented notes, anonymized findings, and research synthesis
- `inspiration/` — broader creative references
- `spatial-capture/` — technical papers, formats, tools, and platform documentation
- `source-register.md` — provenance and relevance index

## Rules

- Prefer a link and a short original summary over copying third-party material.
- Record author or organization, title, URL, access date, relevance, and usage or license notes.
- Distinguish observation from inference.
- Preserve exact quotations only when needed, keep them short, and attribute them.
- Do not commit copyrighted media without permission and a recorded license.
- Do not commit personal customer data or unredacted interview material.
- Use durable filenames and link each stored artifact from the source register.
- Treat time-sensitive claims as stale until reverified.

Research conclusions that become project policy should be reviewed and incorporated into the appropriate context document or decision record.
